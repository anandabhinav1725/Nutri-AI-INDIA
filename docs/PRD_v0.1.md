# PRD v0.1 - Historical Direction

Status: Historical reference
Source basis: Initial concept document, PDF export, and early poster direction
Purpose: Preserve the original product vision and phased feature plan for comparison with the current product direction.

## Vision

Build the easiest way for Indians to track calories, protein, and nutrition using natural conversation instead of traditional calorie-tracking apps.

The initial concept centered on a WhatsApp-based AI nutrition assistant where users could send text, voice notes, or food photos and receive instant calories and macros for Indian meals.

## Problem

Traditional calorie-tracking apps create too much friction for Indian users:

- Food databases are usually optimized for Western foods.
- Indian dishes are missing, inaccurate, or poorly categorized.
- Users must manually search for foods.
- Portion estimation is hard because users rarely know gram weights.
- Logging becomes tedious after a few days.
- Users abandon tracking because the effort is greater than the perceived benefit.

Example foods that motivated the product:

- Khichdi
- Aloo chokha
- Litti chokha
- Sattu drink
- Ghugni
- Chura dahi
- Thekua
- Dal pitha
- Poha
- Upma
- Idli
- Dosa
- Chole bhature

## Original Solution

Create an AI-powered nutrition assistant accessible directly through WhatsApp.

Users would send:

- Text: "2 litti and chokha"
- Voice note: "Aaj maine 3 puri aur aloo ki sabzi khayi."
- Photo: Food image

The AI would:

- Identify food items.
- Estimate portions.
- Calculate calories.
- Calculate protein, carbs, and fats.
- Update daily nutrition totals.

No separate app was required.

## Original Value Proposition

"Text your meals. Get calories instantly."

"The first AI nutrition assistant built specifically for Indian food."

## Historical Version Plan

### Version 1 - MVP

Goal: Validate demand before building a mobile app.

Included:

- WhatsApp chat interface
- Text-based food logging
- Daily calorie tracking
- Daily protein tracking
- Daily nutrition summary
- Indian food database

Example:

User: "Breakfast: 2 bananas and milk"
Assistant: "Calories: 310 kcal. Protein: 11g. Today's total: 310 kcal, 11g protein."

### Version 2

Planned additions:

- Voice note recognition
- Hindi support
- Hinglish support
- Regional food recognition
- Weekly reports

### Version 3

Planned additions:

- Food photo recognition
- Portion size estimation
- Meal history
- Goal tracking
- AI nutrition coach

## Historical Long-Term Vision

Become India's default AI nutrition companion.

Instead of opening a calorie-tracking app, users would message an AI assistant on WhatsApp and receive nutrition insights tailored to Indian food, Indian languages, and Indian lifestyles.

## What Changed After v0.1

The current direction keeps the original vision but sharpens the product principle:

- The product is not primarily a calorie calculator.
- The product solves the effort required to calculate calories.
- Friction reduction, personalization, memory, and trust are now first-class product requirements.
- Voice, photo, coaching, plate calibration, and advanced reports remain important, but they are sequenced behind the simplest validated logging loop.
